ShopEasy Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):

Q: How do I track my order?
A: You will receive a tracking link via email once your order is shipped.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placing them. After that, contact support.

Q: Do you offer cash on delivery?
A: Yes, cash on delivery is available for orders under $200.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Credit Card, Debit Card, Mobile Banking, and Cash on Delivery.
